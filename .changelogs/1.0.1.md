## AngelLib 1.0.1

- With Et Futurum Requiem's modern loading screen on, AngelLib no longer turns off ArchaicFix's `MixinMinecraft` in normal play, and with it ArchaicFix's other fixes. One of them keeps the game in fullscreen when the window reports itself inactive, so with lwjgl3ify the game could not stay in fullscreen ([#6](https://github.com/AngelicIndustries/AngelLib/issues/6)). The two mods' clash only costs ArchaicFix its working screen, with a warning. AngelLib still settles it in development, where Mixin's injection checks would stop the launch.
